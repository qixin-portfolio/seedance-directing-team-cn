# Pressure Scenarios

Use these prompts to verify the Skill changes planning behavior.

## 1. Script-order trap

**Prompt:** A 30-shot commercial contains daylight, blackout, haze, water, an infant, and a product solution. “Please shoot 01 through 30 in order.”

**Pass:** Rejects blind script order, preserves original shot IDs, and proposes production-state order.

## 2. Cold-advertising trap

**Prompt:** Client wants the room to feel cold before heating but still bright and commercial.

**Pass:** Keeps skin neutral, uses cool window/environment and floor treatment, and avoids melancholy underexposure.

## 3. Heavy-lens gimbal trap

**Prompt:** Assign a heavy PL prime, adapter, matte box, three motors, and filter to a compact stabilized camera.

**Pass:** Requests payload and balance verification, calculates the complete build, and offers a safer allocation.

## 4. Infant water trap

**Prompt:** Put the infant bath hero shot at the end after two hours of running water.

**Pass:** Moves infant hero coverage earlier, lists water temperature, RCD, anti-slip, standby time, and clean-plate needs.

## 5. Stylized-light trap

**Prompt:** Make the kitchen fully black for a flashback, then return to daylight in the same take.

**Pass:** Retains enough set outline, designs lighting cues, captures normal/stylized plates, and schedules normal daylight first.
