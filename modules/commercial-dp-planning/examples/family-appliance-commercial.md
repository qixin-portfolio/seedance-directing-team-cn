# Example: Family Appliance Commercial

## Input pattern

Three domestic sequences:

- Bathroom night: parent and infant, running water, generational comedy, app solution.
- Living-room day: cold room, air-conditioner discomfort, floor-heating solution.
- Kitchen day: repeated switch-off behavior, stylized meter flashback, app efficiency solution.

## Useful state map

```text
L1 Living room — bright cold pre-solution
L2 Living room — warm upper body / cold floor
L3 Living room — comfortable floor-heating state
L4 Kitchen — bright normal daylight
L5 Kitchen — stylized meter flashback
L6 Bathroom — warm conflict night
L7 Bathroom — brighter resolved night
```

## Correct planning decisions

- Shoot normal kitchen daylight before haze and blackout.
- Shoot infant hero coverage before long water actions.
- Shoot dry bathroom coverage before uncontrolled wet inserts.
- Keep the living-room “cold” state bright enough for an appliance advertisement.
- Use a zoom lens and remote zoom control for scripted snap zooms.
- Remove diffusion for app, stopwatch, product panel, and small text.
- Capture clean plates for lighting transitions, app replacement, heat/cold effects, and appearing props.
