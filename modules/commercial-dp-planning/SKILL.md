# Commercial DP Planning

## Overview

Turn creative material into a shootable cinematography plan. Prioritize production reality, visual continuity, safety, and schedule over attractive but impractical shot ideas.

## Required Method

1. **Audit inputs.** Separate confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, missing information, and conflicts. Never invent dimensions, daylight direction, equipment compatibility, actor availability, or product UI.
2. **Extract shot facts.** For every shot record scene, action, duration, framing, movement, cast, props, effects, continuity, and post needs.
3. **Build production states.** Group shots by location, lighting state, set state, dry/wet state, cast combination, makeup progression, and mechanical setup.
4. **Test the package.** Check camera mount, lens weight, balance, filter size, focus motors, macro working distance, power, monitoring, and movement support. Do not force expensive equipment into unsuitable work.
5. **Allocate tools.** Assign camera, focal length, movement method, support, filters, and light state to each shot. State why the choice is practical.
6. **Expose risks.** Flag impossible timing, incompatible gear, mirror/reflection issues, screen flicker, water/electricity hazards, infant or elderly constraints, smoke alarms, and missing clean plates.

## Visual Rules

- Cold does not mean blue skin.
- Warm does not mean an orange wash.
- Commercial night does not mean underexposure.
- Product transformation should be visible in lighting, exposure, blocking, or set behavior—not only in grading.
- Use focal length from camera distance and facial perspective, not habit.
- Distinguish a whip pan, follow, push, zoom, orbit, jib, and handheld stop; do not label every moving shot “gimbal.”

## Output Contract

Provide:

1. DP thesis and visual logic.
2. Camera/lens allocation.
3. Numbered lighting states.
4. Shot-by-shot execution table.
5. Equipment gaps and compatibility warnings.
6. Continuity, safety, and post-production requirements.
7. Confidence labels for unverified decisions.

Use the schemas and decision rules in `references/`. For a domestic appliance example, read `examples/family-appliance-commercial.md`.
